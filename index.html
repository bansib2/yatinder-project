About us

Engineering precision since 2007

Yatinder Designs was established in 2007 and has been continuously growing since inception. Our operations conform to ISO 9001, IATF 16949 and ISO 14001 standards.

We manufacture cold forged and machined components for the automotive industry as a Tier-I supplier. We specialize in production of all types of high tensile fasteners, automotive parts, engine, chassis, steering, suspension, brake parts and parts per drawing.

Our manufacturing plant spans 16,000 sq. meters with a built-up area of 12,000 sq. meters. We source steel from Sunflag, Mukund, Tata Steel, Neco Jaiswal, JSPL & JSW.
